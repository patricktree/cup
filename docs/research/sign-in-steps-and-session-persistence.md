# Sign-in steps and session persistence on web, Android and iOS

> Historical research, incorporated 2026-11-11 by the [authentication architecture](../architecture/authentication.md#google-account-chooser), whose per-platform tap targets and known session limits rest on it. [Issue #242](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/issues/242) chose which gaps to fix, and [patricktree/cup#256](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/pull/256) built them. Cup now differs from the state described below: it forces Google's account chooser only after a sign-out or account deletion on the device (gap 1; [ADR 0019](../adr/0019-sign-in-on-ios-through-supabase-oauth-in-a-system-browser-sheet.md), note of 2026-11-10), drives token refresh in the shells from the app's foreground and background events and no longer calls `startAutoRefresh()` before sign-in (gap 6), writes iOS Keychain items with `afterFirstUnlock` (gap 7), and gave the fake auth provider a `refresh_token` grant. Gaps 2, 3, 4, 5 and 9 became Linear follow-ups (CUP-64, CUP-63, CUP-66, CUP-67, CUP-68); gaps 8 and 10 were accepted as known behaviour. External claims have not been reverified.

[Original investigation](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/d1fb0e1eedb46155d5c09ead05654aca02391426/docs/research/sign-in-steps-and-session-persistence.md).

Research date: 2026-11-10, for [issue #238](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/issues/238) of the [home sign-in map](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/issues/236), against the acceptance criteria of Linear [CUP-26](https://linear.app/cup-audio/issue/CUP-26/login-dialog-fast-sign-in-that-keeps-you-signed-in): one tap with Google, and a session that persists so returning listeners need not sign in again. Repository observations are from `main` at [`0a126330`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/tree/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27); library versions are the installed ones: `@supabase/supabase-js` and `@supabase/auth-js` `2.117.3`, `@aparajita/capacitor-secure-storage` `8.0.1` (with `keychain-swift` 21), `@capawesome/capacitor-google-sign-in` `0.1.4`. External documentation was read on 2026-11-10. Nothing was run on a device and no Google account was used, so tap counts are derived from code and documentation, not observed.

## Answer

| Criterion                            | Web                                                                                                          | Android shell                                                                           | iOS shell                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One tap with Google                  | **Not met.** At least 2 taps: "Continue with Google", then Google's account chooser, which Cup always forces | **Not met, nearly.** 2 taps: "Continue with Google", then the account in Google's sheet | **Not met.** At least 3 taps: "Continue with Google", iOS's "wants to use … to Sign In" alert, then the account chooser |
| First sign-in adds                   | Google's consent screen, naming `evwjipxgacwotgbmqtla.supabase.co`                                           | Consent text in Google's sheet                                                          | The same consent screen as the web; Google's password if Safari is not signed in to Google                              |
| Session survives reload/restart/kill | **Yes**, in `localStorage`                                                                                   | **Yes**, in Secure Storage (Android Keystore)                                           | **Yes**, in Secure Storage (Keychain), even across reinstall                                                            |
| What ends it early                   | Safari's 7-day cap on script-writable storage; clearing site data                                            | Reinstall or new device (`allowBackup="false"`)                                         | Suspected: a refresh while the device is locked (unverified)                                                            |

Everywhere: the access token lives 5 minutes (`jwt_exp = 300`) and supabase-js refreshes it; the refresh token never expires but is single-use, and no session time-box or inactivity timeout is configured, so a session lasts until sign-out, refresh-token reuse detection, or loss of the stored copy. The _sign-in dialog_ itself is one tap away only after a URL submission today; the home entry is the map's own subject and is not counted above.

So the persistence criterion holds on all three platforms with the caveats under [Gaps](#gaps-candidate-follow-ups), and the one-tap criterion holds on none. The extra taps come from Cup's choices (forced account chooser, browser flow on iOS, Supabase's own domain on Google's and Apple's screens), not from Google requirements alone.

## How sign-in runs today

The dialog ([`sign-in-dialog.tsx`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/app/components/sign-in-dialog.tsx)) shows one "Continue with Google" button per configured method. [`account-session.ts`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/auth/account-session.ts#L157-L202) loads the public auth configuration, creates the Supabase client with `flowType: "pkce"`, `persistSession: true`, `autoRefreshToken: true` and the platform's storage, and dispatches the Google sign-in to the platform adapter ([`account-auth.ts`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/platform/account-auth.ts)).

### Web

[`account-auth.web.ts`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/platform/account-auth.web.ts#L16-L39) calls `signInWithOAuth` with `queryParams: { prompt: "select_account" }` (plus `max_age: "0"` for a fresh sign-in before account deletion) and redirects the page to Supabase, then Google, then back.

Steps for a returning listener whose browser is signed in to Google: tap "Continue with Google" (page navigates away), tap the account in Google's chooser, page returns signed in. That is 2 taps and 2 full page loads. Google documents `select_account` as "The authorization server prompts the user to select a user account" ([OpenID Connect](https://developers.google.com/identity/openid-connect/openid-connect#authenticationuriparameters)); without a `prompt`, Google shows consent only "if … the user has not previously authorized access" (same page), so a listener with one Google account and earlier consent could skip the chooser. Google's page does not promise the chooser is skipped without `prompt`; `login_hint` is the documented way to skip it ("skips the account chooser", same page).

First sign-in adds Google's consent screen. Without a Supabase custom domain, "users will see `<project-id>.supabase.co`" there, and Supabase "strongly recommend[s]" a custom domain and brand verification ([Supabase Google guide](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/social-login/auth-google)). Terraform configures neither ([`supabase.tf`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/terraform/production/supabase.tf#L38-L49)), and [production configuration](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/docs/production-configuration.md) records none.

### Android shell

[`account-auth.android.ts`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/platform/account-auth.android.ts#L22-L61) calls the Capawesome plugin's `signIn` with a hashed nonce; the plugin builds `GetSignInWithGoogleOption`, the button flow of Credential Manager ([earlier research](native-google-sign-in-testing.md#what-google-checks-before-it-returns-an-id-token)), and Cup exchanges the ID token with `signInWithIdToken`. No page load happens.

Steps: tap "Continue with Google", tap the account in Google's sheet; 2 taps, no browser. Google's other UI, the bottom sheet (`GetGoogleIdOption`), "supports Automatic Sign-In, which removes manual steps for returning users who have already authorized the app", and Google recommends offering both ([Sign in with Google overview](https://developer.android.com/identity/sign-in/credential-manager-siwg#user-interface-guidelines)). The same page says brand verification is needed "for your app name to be visible to users on the Sign in with Google consent screen".

### iOS shell

[`account-auth.ios.ts`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/platform/account-auth.ios.ts#L29-L50) runs the web flow, with the same forced `select_account`, in an `ASWebAuthenticationSession` opened by [`WebAuthenticationPlugin.swift`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/mobile-app/ios/App/App/WebAuthenticationPlugin.swift#L38-L58), which leaves `prefersEphemeralWebBrowserSession` at its default `false` ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/authenticationservices/aswebauthenticationsession/prefersephemeralwebbrowsersession)).

Steps for a listener signed in to Google in Safari: tap "Continue with Google", tap "Continue" on iOS's alert naming the auth provider's host (`evwjipxgacwotgbmqtla.supabase.co`, [ADR 0019](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/docs/adr/0019-sign-in-on-ios-through-supabase-oauth-in-a-system-browser-sheet.md#consequences)), tap the account; at least 3 taps. A listener not signed in to Google in Safari types email, password and any second factor. ADR 0019 rejected the ephemeral session because it "never sees the Google sign-in Safari already has"; that it also skips the alert is the ADR's claim, which Apple's pages read for this research do not state. Native Google sign-in on iOS, which would remove the alert, is blocked by the plugin dropping the nonce (ADR 0019 notes) and is researched in [CUP-63](https://linear.app/cup-audio/issue/CUP-63).

## How the session persists

### Storage

- **Web:** `window.localStorage` under `cup_supabase_session` and its `-code-verifier` key ([`account-auth.web.ts`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/platform/account-auth.web.ts#L4-L15), [`account-session-storage.ts`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/auth/account-session-storage.ts)). It survives reloads and browser restarts. WebKit deletes "all of a website's script-writable storage after seven days of Safari use without user interaction on the site", LocalStorage included; home screen web apps keep their own day count ([WebKit, 7-Day Cap on All Script-Writeable Storage](https://webkit.org/blog/10218/full-third-party-cookie-blocking-and-more/)). A Safari listener who does not open Cup for a week of Safari use is signed out.
- **Android:** Secure Storage ([`account-auth.native.ts`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/platform/account-auth.native.ts)), which encrypts with an AES/GCM key in `AndroidKeyStore` and keeps the ciphertext in the `WSSecureStorageSharedPreferences` shared preferences (plugin `SecureStorage.java`). App data survives restarts and process kills. The manifest sets `android:allowBackup="false"` ([`AndroidManifest.xml`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/mobile-app/android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml#L5)), so a reinstall or a new phone starts signed out.
- **iOS:** Secure Storage in the Keychain. Cup sets no access option, so items get `keychain-swift`'s default `.accessibleWhenUnlocked` (`KeychainSwiftAccessOptions.defaultOption`), which the plugin's own docs describe as "can be accessed only while the device is unlocked". The Keychain outlives the app, so a reinstall stays signed in (ADR 0019).

### Refresh

Terraform sets `jwt_exp = 300` ([`supabase.tf`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/terraform/production/supabase.tf#L43)), and the local stack matches with rotation on and a 10-second reuse interval ([`supabase/config.toml`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/supabase/config.toml#L33-L36)). Supabase says "Values below 5 minutes … should not be used in most situations" and recommends the 1-hour default for most apps ([Supabase sessions](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/sessions)); five minutes is at that floor.

auth-js ticks every 30 seconds and refreshes when the token expires within 3 ticks (90 seconds) (`AUTO_REFRESH_TICK_DURATION_MS`, `AUTO_REFRESH_TICK_THRESHOLD` in `@supabase/auth-js` `lib/constants.js`); every `getSession()`, which each API request makes through `getFreshAccountSession`, also refreshes inside that margin. In a browser the client runs the ticker only while `document.visibilityState` is `visible` and recovers the session on return to visible (`_onVisibilityChanged`). Cup's sign-in actor calls `startAutoRefresh()` before every sign-in ([`account-session-machine.ts`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/auth/account-session-machine.ts#L115-L127)), and auth-js's `startAutoRefresh` first calls `_removeVisibilityChangedCallback()`; its doc comment says it is "Only useful in non-browser environments". On the web the OAuth redirect reloads the page, so this does not last; in both shells, where sign-in completes in place, the ticker then runs regardless of visibility and no foreground recovery happens until the next cold start. Sign-out calls `stopAutoRefresh()`, which also removes the callback.

A failed refresh keeps the session while the access token is still valid and removes it once it has expired and the refresh token is rejected (`_callRefreshToken`); retryable network errors do not remove it.

### What ends a session

Supabase: "refresh tokens never expire but can only be used once"; sessions end on sign-out, password change or another security-sensitive action, inactivity timeout, maximum lifetime, or single-session-per-user; time-box and inactivity timeout are "only available on Pro Plans and up" ([Supabase sessions](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/sessions)). Terraform sets none of them, so no lifetime limit applies. Reusing a refresh token outside the 10-second reuse interval marks "all refresh tokens belonging to it … as revoked" (same page). Cup's sign-out calls `signOut({ scope: "local" })`, clears the stored keys and, on Android, signs out of Google on the device ([`account-session-machine.ts`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/auth/account-session-machine.ts#L128-L135)).

Cold starts restore the stored session only after fetching `/api/auth/config` (`loadConnection` in [`account-session.ts`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/web-app/src/auth/account-session.ts#L157-L174)); when that request fails, the machine enters `error` and the UI shows the auth error rather than the stored account ([authentication architecture](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/docs/architecture/authentication.md#client-session-lifecycle)). The session is not lost, but a returning listener without a connection does not appear signed in.

## Gaps (candidate follow-ups)

1. **Forced account chooser on every non-fresh sign-in (web, iOS).** `googleAuthorizationParams` always sends `prompt=select_account`, adding a tap for returning listeners with one Google account. Dropping it for non-fresh sign-ins, or passing `login_hint` for a known returning account, could save a tap; the trade-off is ADR 0019's reliance on the chooser to switch accounts after Cup's sign-out, since Safari's Google session stays.
2. **Supabase's host on Google's consent screen and iOS's alert (all platforms).** No Supabase custom domain and, as far as the repository records, no Google brand verification; first-time listeners see `evwjipxgacwotgbmqtla.supabase.co`. This does not add a tap but undermines the single-tap flow's trust.
3. **iOS needs at least 3 taps.** The `ASWebAuthenticationSession` alert is unavoidable with a shared-cookie session; removing it means native Google sign-in on iOS (CUP-63, blocked on the nonce), or an ephemeral session, which costs the Safari Google sign-in.
4. **Android uses only the button flow.** Google's bottom sheet with automatic sign-in for authorized accounts is not used; with a persisting session this matters mainly after a reinstall, new device or sign-out.
5. **Safari's 7-day cap ends web sessions (web).** A Safari listener idle for seven days of Safari use loses `localStorage` and must sign in again. Mitigations to evaluate: an installed home screen web app, or keeping the refresh token in a server-set first-party cookie.
6. **Native shells lose the visibility-driven refresh after an in-app sign-in.** `startAutoRefresh()` before each sign-in removes auth-js's visibility handling, so the shells refresh in the background and skip the foreground recovery. Supabase's documented pattern for non-browser apps is to call `startAutoRefresh`/`stopAutoRefresh` from the app's foreground and background events, which Cup does not do.
7. **Suspected iOS session loss on a locked device (unverified).** The Keychain items are `whenUnlocked`, the app has the `audio` background mode ([`Info.plist`](https://github.com/patricktree/cup/blob/0a1263302b16246f493c4802ed99e35f70756d27/apps/mobile-app/ios/App/App/Info.plist#L34-L37)), and gap 6 keeps the ticker running in the background. If a refresh succeeds at Supabase while the phone is locked, the rotated session cannot be written to the Keychain; if the app is then killed, the next start presents the consumed refresh token outside the reuse interval, which revokes the session. Needs a device test before any fix; `afterFirstUnlock` access would remove the premise.
8. **Five-minute access tokens.** At Supabase's stated floor; with a short token every background or offline stretch longer than five minutes depends on a refresh. Not a persistence failure today, but it amplifies gaps 6 and 7.
9. **Offline cold start shows an auth error instead of the stored account (all platforms).** The configuration fetch gates session restoration.
10. **Android reinstall or new device starts signed out.** `allowBackup="false"` keeps the Keystore-bound ciphertext out of backups, which is consistent with Keystore keys not migrating; CUP-26 may accept this, but it is the Android counterpart of iOS's Keychain persistence.
